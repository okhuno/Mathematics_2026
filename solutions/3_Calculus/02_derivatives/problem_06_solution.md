### Problem 6. Product Rule and Trigonometry
For

```math
f(x)=x\sin x+\cos x
```

compute the derivative and simplify the result. Then compute $`f'(0)`$ and interpret the direction of the tangent line at that point.

> **Why this problem:** it combines the product rule with trigonometric derivatives and geometric interpretation.

#### Answer
- $`f'(x)=x\cos x`$
- $`f'(0)=0`$: the tangent at $`x=0`$ is horizontal (the line $`y=1`$).

#### How I solved it
- **Product rule on $`x\sin x`$:** $`1\cdot\sin x+x\cdot\cos x`$.
- **Add the derivative of $`\cos x`$:** $`-\sin x`$.
- **Sum:** $`\sin x+x\cos x-\sin x=x\cos x`$ (the $`\sin x`$ terms cancel).
- $`f'(0)=0\cdot\cos0=0`$.

**Interpretation:** slope $`0`$ means a horizontal tangent. Since $`f(0)=0+\cos0=1`$, the tangent line is $`y=1`$.
