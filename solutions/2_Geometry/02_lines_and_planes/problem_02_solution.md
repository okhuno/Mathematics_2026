### Exercise 2. A Line in Space
Given

```math
L:(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)+t(1,2,-2).
```

State a point on the line and its direction vector, check whether $`P=(4,3,-1)`$ lies on $`L`$, and find the point corresponding to $`t=-1`$.

> **Why this exercise:** reinforces the interpretation of parametric form as “point + direction.”

#### Answer
- Point: $`(2,-1,3)`$; direction: $`(1,2,-2)`$.
- $`P=(4,3,-1)`$ lies on $`L`$ (it corresponds to $`t=2`$).
- $`t=-1`$ gives $`(1,-3,5)`$.

#### How I solved it
- **Read it:** "start at $`(2,-1,3)`$, move along $`(1,2,-2)`$."
- **Is $`P`$ on the line?** We need one value of $`t`$ that works for all three coordinates:
  - $`x`$: $`2+t=4\Rightarrow t=2`$
  - $`y`$: $`-1+2t=3\Rightarrow t=2`$
  - $`z`$: $`3-2t=-1\Rightarrow t=2`$

  All three agree, so yes.
- **$`t=-1`$:** $`(2-1,\ -1-2,\ 3+2)=(1,-3,5)`$.
