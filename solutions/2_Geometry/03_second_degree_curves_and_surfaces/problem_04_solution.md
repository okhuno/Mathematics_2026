### Exercise 4. Parabola
For

```math
y^2=12x,
```

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find points on the parabola for $`x=3`$ and use them to make a sketch.

> **Why this exercise:** teaches how to move from the equation of a parabola to its basic geometry.

#### Answer
- Vertex $`(0,0)`$, axis of symmetry: the $`x`$-axis, opens to the right.
- For $`x=3`$: points $`(3,6)`$ and $`(3,-6)`$.

#### How I solved it
- $`y^2=4px`$ with $`4p=12`$, so $`p=3`$ (focus $`(3,0)`$).
- Since $`y^2\ge0`$, we need $`x\ge0`$: the curve opens to the right. Replacing $`y`$ by $`-y`$ does not change the equation, so it is symmetric about the $`x`$-axis.
- For $`x=3`$: $`y^2=36`$, so $`y=\pm6`$.
- Sketch (see the middle picture above): start at the origin and draw the curve through $`(3,6)`$ and $`(3,-6)`$.
