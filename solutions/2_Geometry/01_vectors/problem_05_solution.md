### Exercise 5. Angle Between Vectors
Given $`u=(1,1,0)`$ and $`v=(1,0,1)`$:

1. Use the sign of $`u\cdot v`$ to predict the type of angle.
2. Compute the angle.
3. Compare the result with the prediction.

> **Why this exercise:** teaches how to extract qualitative information first and only then perform the exact calculation.

#### Answer
- $`u\cdot v=1>0`$, so the angle is predicted to be **acute**.
- $`\cos\theta=\frac12`$, so $`\theta=60^\circ`$ (acute, matches the prediction ✓).

#### How I solved it
**Quick rule from the sign of the dot product:** positive means acute (less than $`90^\circ`$), zero means right angle, negative means obtuse (more than $`90^\circ`$).

1. **Prediction:** $`u\cdot v=1\cdot1+1\cdot0+0\cdot1=1`$. Positive, so acute.
2. **Lengths:** $`\|u\|=\sqrt{1+1+0}=\sqrt2`$ and $`\|v\|=\sqrt{1+0+1}=\sqrt2`$.
3. **Cosine:** $`\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2}=\dfrac12`$.
4. **Angle:** the angle whose cosine is $`\frac12`$ is $`60^\circ`$.
5. **Compare:** $`60^\circ<90^\circ`$, so it is acute, as predicted.
